Summary

TrulyChat is a lightweight chat built around channel numbers. Messages are visible to people in the same channel. Do not share sensitive information.

What we store

  • Channel messages sent by participants.
  • Your chosen display name for the session.
  • Basic timestamps to keep chats ordered.

What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or account profile data.
  • No private contact info required.
  • No hidden tracking for personal identity.

How data is used

Messages are stored in the channel database so participants can see them in real time. Your name and messages are visible to anyone in the same channel number.

Share your channel number only with people you want in the room.

Your choices

  • Change your display name from the menu during a session.
  • Leave the channel at any time to stop receiving updates.
  • Use a new channel number to start a fresh conversation.
