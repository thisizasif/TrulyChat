About

Private rooms you can start in seconds.

TrulyChat is a minimal, channel-based chat built for quick, private conversations without accounts or setup.

Join a channel Back to chat

How it works

  1. Pick a name Choose a display name for the session.
  2. Enter a channel number Share the number with people you trust.
  3. Start talking Messages appear instantly for everyone online.

Why channel numbers?

They keep rooms simple, memorable, and fast to join. No invites, no accounts, just a shared number.

When to use a new number

Use a fresh channel when you want a clean room or a new group of people.

Designed for

Study groups

Spin up a room for quick collaboration.

Events

Coordinate guests without adding contacts.

Friends

Share a number and chat instantly.

Keep it private

Share your channel number only with people you want in the room. Avoid posting sensitive info in public spaces.
