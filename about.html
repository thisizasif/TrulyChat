What is TrulyChat?

TrulyChat is a fast, private way to talk in temporary rooms. Pick a channel number, share it, and chat live without creating an account.

How it works

  • Choose a display name for the session.
  • Enter a channel number to join the room.
  • Messages appear instantly for everyone online.

Why channel numbers?

  • Easy to remember and share.
  • No invitations or sign-ups required.
  • Great for quick groups and one-off chats.
